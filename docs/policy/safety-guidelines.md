## 1. For everyone — the basics (en, 번역 필요)

> **SeOUL MATE is a free volunteer community, not a tour company.** Volunteers are local residents who want to share their city. They are not licensed tour guides, and we don't run background checks. Please use the same care you would when meeting anyone new.

**First meeting checklist**
1. **Meet in a busy public place** — a subway station exit, a café, or a well-known landmark. Never meet first at a home, hotel room or private car.
2. **Meet in daylight** for your first meeting, and keep it to the time you agreed in the request.
3. **Tell someone you trust** where you're going, who you're meeting and when you'll be back.
4. **Keep chatting in SeOUL MATE until you're matched.** Contact details are shared only after the traveler accepts a guide.
5. **Have your own way home.** Know the nearest subway station and keep your phone charged.
6. **Go easy on alcohol**, especially the first time you meet. You can always leave.
7. **Trust your feelings.** If something feels wrong, you can end the meeting at any time. You don't need to give a reason.

**No money, gifts or tips — ever**
- Volunteers do not accept payment, tips, gifts or "thank-you" money.
- **Everyone pays their own way**: food, drinks, tickets and transport. Splitting the bill is normal in Korea.
- Travelers: please don't offer to pay for your guide. Guides: please don't let travelers pay for you.
- Nobody should ask you for money, a loan, a deposit or a "booking fee". If someone does, stop and report it.

**Respect**
- No harassment, sexual comments or pressure to go somewhere you don't want to go.
- Ask before taking photos of someone, and ask again before posting them.
- Don't post where someone is *right now*. Share photos after you've said goodbye.

## 2. For travelers (en, 번역 필요)

- **Your request is visible to volunteers.** Volunteers who are signed in can see your name, photo, nationality, travel date and area. Don't put your hotel name, room number or flight details in the request.
- **Choose your guide in the chat.** Ask about their plan, which languages they speak and where to meet. Accept only someone you feel comfortable with.
- **Accepting shares contact details.** When you accept a guide, you each see the other's contact details. If your plans change, you can reopen your request. They will stop seeing your details in the app, but they may have already saved them.
- **Keep your passport, cards and cash with you.** Never hand them to anyone.
- **Plans changed? Tell your guide in the chat** as early as possible, ideally at least 2 hours before you meet.
- **No-show:** If your guide doesn't arrive within 20 minutes and doesn't reply, it's fine to leave. Repeated no-shows can be reported with "Something else".

## 3. For volunteer guides (en, 번역 필요)

- **You're a local friend, not a tour guide.** Don't call yourself a licensed or professional guide, and don't sell or advertise paid tours, transport or shopping trips.
- **Never accept money, tips, gifts, or let the traveler pay for your meals, drinks or tickets.** Paying your own way keeps this volunteering, and it matters legally. Under Korean tourism law, guiding foreign tourists for pay can require a tourist interpretation guide licence.
- **No commissions.** Don't take travelers to shops, restaurants or businesses that pay you, or that you own or work for, unless you tell them clearly first.
- **No rides.** Don't drive travelers in your car. Use public transport or a taxi, and split the fare.
- **Respect the traveler's choice.** If they choose another guide or don't reply, don't keep messaging them.
- **Be on time.** If you can't make it, tell the traveler in the chat as early as possible, ideally at least 2 hours before. Not showing up without notice may lead to a warning.
- **Know the emergency numbers** (section 5) so you can help a traveler who gets hurt, lost or sick.
- **Your safety matters too.** The same checklist applies to you: public place, tell a friend, leave if you feel uncomfortable.

---

## 4. If something goes wrong (en, 번역 필요)

1. **If you're in danger, leave and call 112 first.** Go somewhere with people around: a convenience store, a station or a café.
2. **Report it in the app.** Open the chat with that person → tap **Report** at the top → choose what happened → add details (optional, up to 1,000 characters) → **Send report**.
   - You can report someone after they have sent at least one message in that chat.
   - You can report the same person once per chat, and send up to 10 reports a day.
   - When you report, we keep a copy of the recent messages in that chat so we can review what happened, even if they delete their account.
   - We won't tell the person that you reported them. If we warn or suspend them, though, they may be able to guess who reported them.
   - Reports are reviewed by the SeOUL MATE team. **[응답 목표 시간 — `moderation.md` 확정 후 기입. 확정 전에는 시간을 약속하지 않는다]**
3. **Stop contact.** You don't have to reply. If you've already shared contact details, block them in that messenger app (KakaoTalk, WhatsApp, LINE, Instagram). *(Blocking inside SeOUL MATE is not available yet. 차단 기능이 생기면 이 문장을 바꾼다.)*
4. **Keep evidence.** Don't delete the chat. Screenshots of messages in other apps help us review your report.

## 5. Emergency numbers in Korea (en, 번역 필요)

| Number | What for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **112** | Police: crime, threats, harassment, feeling unsafe | Free from any phone |
| **119** | Fire, ambulance, medical emergency | Free from any phone |
| **1330** | Korea Travel Hotline: travel help and interpretation | From abroad: +82-2-1330. Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese are reported to be available 24 hours. Other languages (e.g. Russian, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay/Indonesian) are reported to have shorter hours. **확인 필요**: 자료마다 운영 시간이 달라서 한국관광공사 공식 페이지로 확정해야 함 |

You can also contact your country's embassy in Seoul if you lose your passport or need consular help.

## 6. 정책 근거 메모 (내부용, 사용자 비노출)

- **금전·선물·팁 금지와 "각자 계산"**: 관광진흥법은 무자격자가 외국인 관광객 대상 관광통역안내를 하는 것을 제한한다. 2026-05-12 시행 현행법 제86조 제1항 제2호는 "제38조제6항을 위반하여 관광통역안내를 한 자"에게 500만원 이하 과태료를 정한다([국가법령정보센터 조문정보](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLawLinkInfo.do?lsJoLnkSeq=1000835294&chrClsCd=010202), 2026-10-17 확인). 제38조 제6항의 정확한 문언과 적용 범위(여행업자를 통한 안내에 한정되는지, 무보수 자원봉사에도 적용되는지)는 원문을 확인하지 못했다 → **확인 필요**(질문 목록: `review-free-promotion.md` 5장). 보수뿐 아니라 식사·입장료 부담 같은 "사실상 대가"도 위험을 키울 수 있다고 보고 금지했다(**가설**, 전문가 확인 필요).
- **"커피 한 잔도 안 되나"**: 소액의 예외를 두면 경계가 흐려지므로 "각자 계산"을 원칙으로 한다. 사회 통념상 허용되는 범위가 있는지는 전문가 확인 항목으로 둔다.
- **차량 동승 금지**: 무상이어도 사고 시 책임과 안전 문제가 크다. 운송 관련 법령 판단은 하지 않았다(확인 필요 아님, 안전 정책으로서의 금지).
- **"background checks를 하지 않는다"**: 현재 가이드 신원 확인이 없으므로(`src/app/profile/actions.ts:22-25`, 역할 자기 선택) 사실대로 알린다. 과장된 안전 보장을 막는다.
